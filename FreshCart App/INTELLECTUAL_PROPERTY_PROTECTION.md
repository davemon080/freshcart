### 2. EXCLUSIVE PROPRIETARY INVENTIONS & PROTECTED CLAIMS

Any unauthorized reproduction, adoption, commercial deployment, or derivation of the following core systems and proprietary mechanisms is strictly prohibited:

#### 2.1 The Hyper-Local LGA Geofence Invariant & Dynamic Radius Scaling
* **Proprietary Claim:** The system architecture restricting produce sourcing, inventory matching, and courier dispatch exclusively within single Local Government Area (LGA) boundaries ($< 7\text{km}$ transit radius) to eliminate cross-city transport spoilage.
* **Dynamic Peak Radius Contraction:** The algorithmic mechanism that dynamically shrinks the permissible delivery radius from $10\text{km}$ down to $5\text{km}$ during peak rush hours (4:00 PM – 7:30 PM) combined with a $1.25\times$ peak dispatch multiplier to preserve the 45-minute fulfillment SLA.

#### 2.2 Hub-and-Spoke Traditional Open-Air Market Booth Aggregation Architecture
* **Proprietary Claim:** The physical-to-digital aggregation model stationing dedicated market agents and calibrated digital scale booths inside major informal open-air markets (e.g., Utako Market, Garki Model Market, Wuse Market) as digital aggregation nodes, bypassing the requirement for individual informal traders to operate smartphones.

#### 2.3 Nigerian Local Produce Metric Digital Standardization Framework
* **Proprietary Claim:** The proprietary transformation framework that translates non-standard open-air Nigerian market measures into verified digital metrics with strict quality tolerance invariants:
  * **Fresh Vegetables:** Traditional *"Tie" / Bunch* $\rightarrow$ Standardized 500g Cleaned & Tied Bundle (washed, root-trimmed, zero yellowing leaves).
  * **Raw Tomatoes / Peppers:** Traditional *"Painter Bucket" / "Basket"* $\rightarrow$ Exact 2.5kg / 5.0kg Digital Calibrated Weight ($\le 2\%$ softness tolerance, zero decay).
  * **Fresh Meat & Poultry:** Traditional *"Kilo" / "Quarter"* $\rightarrow$ Net 1.0kg Vacuum-Sealed chilled at 4°C in dual-compartment thermal boxes.
  * **Grains & Staples:** Traditional *"Mudu" / "Derica"* $\rightarrow$ Standardized 1.5kg Sealed Moisture-Proof Pouch ($< 12\%$ moisture, machine-cleaned, de-stoned).
  * **Tubers & Roots:** Traditional *"Heap of 5"* $\rightarrow$ Weight Grade (3.0kg+ Large Tuber with unbroken skin).

#### 2.4 The Zero-Friction 4-Digit Security PIN Handshake Protocol
* **Proprietary Claim:** The two-sided cryptographic verification handshake where:
  1. A unique 4-digit numeric code is generated upon digital escrow pre-payment, accessible and visible **solely** on the buyer’s device.
  2. Enforces the strict compliance invariant: `No PIN = No Handover`.
  3. Dispatch riders must enter this buyer PIN into an in-app numeric keypad to mark deliveries complete.
  4. Incorporates a 3-consecutive-attempt lockout mechanism that freezes the transaction, holds the rider queue, and alerts the LGA Operations Lead.
  5. Mandates physical doorstep inspection of the tamper-evident barcode seal prior to PIN release.

#### 2.5 100% Pre-Payment Escrow & Automated Twice-Daily Split Settlement Engine
* **Proprietary Claim:** The complete elimination and disabling of Cash-on-Delivery (COD) for raw perishable food logistics to neutralize robbery and fake SMS bank alert scams.
* **Automated Split Formula:** Automated algorithm splitting pre-paid escrow upon PIN verification:
  * $80\%$ of gross food produce value remitted to partner market vendors.
  * $80\%$ of delivery fee credited immediately to rider wallets.
  * Remaining margin allocated to packaging COGS, payment gateway fees, and FreshCart net gross profit ($10.2\%$).
* **Twice-Daily Automated Bank Batch Payouts:** Automated batch settlement triggered at 12:00 PM mid-day and 6:00 PM evening to maintain daily cash-flow liquidity for market traders.

#### 2.6 Multi-Tier Quality Enforcement & Vendor 3-Strike Governance
* **Proprietary Claim:** The governance framework that enforces:
  * Vendor 3-strike escalation (3 strikes on spoiled or bait-and-switch goods = permanent platform ban).
  * Doorstep broken-seal rider penalties ($100\%$ fine of delivery fee).
  * Mandatory Return-to-Booth Protocol requiring rejected packages to be returned to the market booth for agent re-inspection before refunds are processed.
